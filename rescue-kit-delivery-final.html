Get started in 3 steps

  1. Click the big button above. The kit opens in a new tab. No app or login needed.
  2. Bookmark THIS page. Press Ctrl+D (or Cmd+D on a Mac), or use "Add to Home Screen" on your phone. Come back here any time to open your kit.
  3. Pick tonight's dinner. Choose how tired you are, tap a recipe, and tap "I made this" when you cook it.
